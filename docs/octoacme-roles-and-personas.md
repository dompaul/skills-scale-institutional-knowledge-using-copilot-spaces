# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Engineer / Quality Specialist

### Role Summary
QA Engineers ensure that features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria before release. They develop test strategies, execute test plans, and collaborate with developers and product teams to identify and resolve defects early.

### Responsibilities
- Review acceptance criteria and feature specifications for testability
- Develop and maintain test plans, test cases, and test automation
- Execute manual and automated tests across different environments
- Report, track, and verify defects with clear reproduction steps
- Participate in sprint planning and retrospectives to identify quality improvements
- Conduct exploratory testing and regression testing before releases
- Validate that features meet accessibility and performance requirements

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Reduce mean time to detect and resolve issues
- Increase test coverage and automation
- Ensure acceptance criteria are clear and testable

### Interactions
- Daily collaboration with developers during feature implementation
- Review PRs for test coverage and testability
- QA sign-off before features move to staging or production
- Sprint planning and retrospectives with the full team
- Bug triage sessions with PM and engineering leads

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers create intuitive, accessible, and visually cohesive user interfaces. They collaborate with product managers, developers, and stakeholders to translate user needs into design solutions that enhance user experience and meet business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Design user interfaces, wireframes, mockups, and prototypes
- Conduct user research, usability testing, and gather feedback
- Collaborate with PMs to understand requirements and user workflows
- Work with developers to ensure design feasibility and implementation accuracy
- Create and maintain design systems and style guides
- Ensure designs meet accessibility standards (WCAG, ARIA)
- Validate design solutions through user testing and iterate based on feedback

### Goals
- Deliver user-centered designs that improve usability and satisfaction
- Maintain visual and interaction consistency across products
- Reduce friction in user flows and increase task completion rates
- Ensure accessibility compliance for all users

### Interactions
- Early collaboration with PMs during feature ideation and requirements
- Design reviews with stakeholders and development teams
- Handoff sessions with developers to clarify design specifications
- User testing sessions with support and customer success teams
- Participation in retrospectives to improve design-development workflow

---

## System Administrator / DevOps

### Role Summary
System Administrators and DevOps engineers manage infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, deployments, and system reliability. They ensure that development and production environments are secure, scalable, and performant, enabling teams to deliver software efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Manage CI/CD pipelines for automated builds, tests, and deployments
- Configure and maintain development, staging, and production environments
- Implement security configurations, access controls, and compliance measures
- Monitor system health, performance, and reliability (SLAs/SLOs)
- Coordinate deployments and provide operational support during releases
- Automate infrastructure provisioning and configuration management
- Respond to incidents, outages, and performance degradation

### Goals
- Maintain high system availability and reliability
- Reduce deployment friction and cycle time
- Ensure security and compliance across all environments
- Enable teams to self-serve infrastructure needs safely

### Interactions
- Deployment planning and coordination with PM and engineering teams
- Incident response and escalation with on-call developers and support
- Security and compliance reviews with security teams
- Capacity planning discussions with product and engineering leads
- Post-deployment verification and rollback coordination

---

## Subject Matter Expert (SME)

### Role Summary
Subject Matter Experts provide specialized domain knowledge to ensure that product features align with business rules, industry standards, and regulatory requirements. They validate requirements, clarify complex business logic, and support adoption and training.

### Responsibilities
- Provide domain expertise and validate business requirements
- Review and approve technical specifications for accuracy and compliance
- Clarify business rules, workflows, and data models
- Support user acceptance testing (UAT) and validation
- Identify edge cases and scenarios that require special handling
- Assist in training and onboarding end users on new features
- Bridge communication between technical teams and business stakeholders

### Goals
- Ensure features meet business and regulatory requirements
- Reduce rework caused by misunderstood requirements
- Support successful adoption of new functionality
- Maintain alignment between technical implementation and business intent

### Interactions
- Requirements gathering sessions with PMs and business analysts
- Design and architecture reviews for domain-specific validation
- User acceptance testing with QA and product teams
- Knowledge transfer sessions with development and support teams
- Stakeholder briefings to communicate business impact

---

## Customer Support / Success

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Success teams represent the voice of the customer, providing feedback on user needs, escalating issues, and ensuring smooth onboarding and ongoing support. They coordinate with delivery teams to resolve incidents and improve product usability.

### Responsibilities
- Provide front-line support to end users via tickets, chat, or phone
- Escalate product defects, feature requests, and usability issues to engineering
- Coordinate with delivery teams during incidents and outages
- Gather and communicate customer feedback to inform product priorities
- Assist with user onboarding, training, and documentation
- Track and report on support trends, SLA adherence, and customer satisfaction
- Validate fixes and new features from an end-user perspective

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and reduce time-to-resolution
- Ensure seamless communication between customers and product teams
- Identify patterns in support issues to drive product improvements
- Support successful customer adoption and retention

### Interactions
- Daily coordination with engineering and QA on escalated issues
- Incident response collaboration with DevOps and on-call teams
- Feature validation sessions with PMs and UX designers
- Weekly syncs with PM to review support trends and feedback
- Knowledge base and documentation collaboration with technical writers
